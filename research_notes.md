## Effective Prompting Techniques

### Beginner Techniques
1. **Zero-Shot Prompting**
   - Ask direct questions without examples
   - Example: "Write a short poem about autumn."

2. **One-Shot Prompting**
   - Include a single example to guide the AI
   - Example: "Here's an example of a product description: [example]. Now write a similar one for [new product]."

3. **Basic Instructions**
   - Clear, simple instructions
   - Example: "Summarize this paragraph in three sentences."

### Intermediate Techniques
1. **Few-Shot Prompting**
   - Provide multiple examples to establish patterns
   - Example: "Here are three examples of marketing emails: [examples]. Write a similar email for our new product launch."

2. **Contextual Prompting**
   - Provide detailed background information
   - Example: "I'm a marketing manager at a tech startup targeting small businesses. Help me create content that addresses their pain points around [specific issue]."

3. **Role-Based Prompting**
   - Assign a specific role to the AI
   - Example: "Act as an experienced data scientist and explain how to approach this clustering problem."

### Advanced Techniques
1. **Chain of Thought (CoT) Prompting**
   - Guide the AI through step-by-step reasoning
   - Example: "Let's solve this problem step by step. First, identify the key variables..."

2. **Tree of Thoughts (ToT)**
   - Explore multiple reasoning paths simultaneously
   - Example: "Consider three different approaches to solving this problem. For each approach, outline the steps, potential challenges, and expected outcomes."

3. **Prompt Chaining**
   - Use outputs from one prompt as inputs to another
   - Example: "First, generate a list of product features. Then, for each feature, create a benefit statement that explains its value to customers."

4. **Iterative Refinement**
   - Systematically improve outputs through feedback
   - Example: "This is good, but make it more concise and focus more on the technical aspects."

## Web Design Notes

### Color Palette Suggestions
- **Primary Color**: White (#FFFFFF) - 60% of the design
- **Secondary Colors**: 
  - Light Blue (#E6F3FF) - 30% of the design
  - Medium Blue (#4A90E2) - For buttons and important elements
  - Dark Blue (#2C3E50) - For text and headers
- **Accent Color**: Bright Blue (#1E88E5) - 10% of the design, for highlights and call-to-action elements

### Typography Recommendations
- **Headers**: Sans-serif fonts like Montserrat or Roboto
- **Body Text**: Clean, readable fonts like Open Sans or Lato
- **Font Sizes**:
  - H1: 32-36px
  - H2: 24-28px
  - H3: 20-22px
  - Body: 16-18px
- **Font Weights**:
  - Headers: Bold (700)
  - Subheaders: Semi-bold (600)
  - Body: Regular (400)

### Layout Principles
1. **Generous White Space**
   - Allow content to breathe with ample margins and padding
   - Use a grid system with consistent spacing

2. **Clear Visual Hierarchy**
   - Establish importance through size, weight, and color
   - Use consistent heading styles across all pages

3. **Minimalist Navigation**
   - Simple, clear menu structure
   - Highlight current page in navigation

4. **Responsive Design**
   - Fluid layouts that adapt to different screen sizes
   - Mobile-first approach to ensure accessibility

5. **Content Organization**
   - Group related information
   - Use cards or sections with subtle shadows or borders
